Research Summary: Neural Networks and Transformer Attention

Key Architecture:
- Artificial Neurons: activation functions (ReLU, Sigmoid, GELU).
- Multilayer Perceptrons: forward pass matrix multiplication z = WX + b.
- Backpropagation and Chain Rule: calculating error gradients across computational graphs.
- Transformer Architecture: Positional Encoding, Multi-Head Attention, and Feed-Forward Networks.
- Query, Key, and Value: Scaled dot-product attention formula:
  Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k))V
- Prompt Engineering and In-Context Learning: zero-shot, few-shot, and Chain-of-Thought (CoT).
